# Personal Ledger Manager

Team **LSH26-T020** · Problem **P12**

Personal Ledger Manager helps users record monthly income and expenses, understand their spending, forecast their month-end balance, and plan savings for specific goals.

## Required features

- Set a monthly salary and add expenses manually.
- Upload a bill or receipt and extract its amount, date, and shop name.
- Review and correct every extracted field before saving.
- View total spending against salary, category breakdowns, largest expenses, and month-over-month changes.
- Forecast remaining-month spending and the expected month-end surplus or shortfall.
- Generate at least three insights containing specific categories and amounts.
- Create savings pockets with a name, item details, target, and monthly contribution.
- Show forecast-based completion dates and projected DPS returns at a stated rate.

## Planned technology

- Next.js and TypeScript
- Node.js and Express
- MongoDB and Mongoose
- Firebase Authentication
- Vercel

The codebase will follow a feature-based modular pattern. No credentials or personal data should be committed. Local secrets must be supplied through ignored environment files and deployment settings.
